# Phase 9 — Raspberry Pi Power (12V → DC Jack)

The Penta SATA Hat includes a DC barrel jack input that accepts 12V and provides regulated power to both the Hat and, through it, the Raspberry Pi 5. This eliminates the need for a separate USB-C power supply for the Pi.

**Steps:**

1. **Identify the 12V rail** from the ATX PSU:
   - On the ATX 24-pin connector or a spare Molex/SATA power connector, the **yellow wires** carry **+12V**.
   - **Black wires** are GND.
   - Verify with a multimeter: yellow (+) to black (−) should read ~12V DC.

2. **Tap a 12V source** — use a spare Molex power connector or SATA power connector from the PSU. Do not cut the main 24-pin bundle.

3. **Prepare the Male DC barrel jack:**
   - The Penta SATA Hat uses a standard **5.5mm/2.1mm DC barrel jack** (centre-positive).
   - Solder the **yellow (+12V) wire** to the **centre pin (positive)** of the male DC jack.
   - Solder the **black (GND) wire** to the **outer barrel (negative)**.
   - Apply shrink tubing to both solder joints individually, then an outer layer over the full connector.

4. **Plug the DC jack** into the power input on the Penta SATA Hat.

5. **Verify polarity once more** with a multimeter before powering on — reversed polarity will damage the Hat and the Pi.

> ⚠️ **Centre-positive:** Confirm the Penta SATA Hat's DC input is centre-positive before wiring. Check the Geekworm product page or silkscreen marking on the board (usually a diagram showing + in the centre circle).
